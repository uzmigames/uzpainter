**Status**: accepted
**Date**: 2026-11-03
**Related Tasks**: phase2.9_pilot-belt, phase3.1_export-island-padding

## Context

The Sash v5 (`25-Faixa-Cintura` v5) is the phase2.9 pilot since decision 260. Its round folder (`_tmp/claude/cintura-v5/`: both arguments files, the 19 masks and `T_sash`) and its two swatch originals are lost, and no golden of the original round was ever recorded (decisions 192, 260; `parity/reports/phase2/sash-v5-input-inventory.md`). Decision 300 item 7 prepared a pinned current round, `phase2/tier1/sash-v5-current`: the low, the AO, the 19 masks and the colour producer were all made and reproduced (task notes §13 to §16, front W's `tools/parity/tier1/sash_colour.py`). Two things were missing: the two swatches, which needed the owner's authorization for an image generator, and his yes to a changed baseline (decision 300 item 8, phase2.9 item 2.8).

Like the Wand and the Tunic, the Sash is UV-islanded, so production refuses its five-map export: the Normal on the islands (decision 245) and the `infinite` padding there (decision 259). Decision 396 §4 leaves each pilot to rely on an owner exception of its own, through the measure export, or to wait on `phase3.1_export-island-padding`.

## Decision

1. **The changed baseline** (decision 300 item 8, phase2.9 item 2.8).
   - The Sash's Tier 1 comparison basis is the re-run waist-v5 round, the admitted golden `phase2/tier1/sash-v5-current` (golden record `2cc1ba7e…`, commit 376b0e88).
   - `phase2/tier1/sash-v5`, the original script golden, stays unrecordable: its inputs are lost. The migration golden `phase2/tier1/sash-v5-migration` is retained unchanged as the delivered project's reference. A Sash result is reported against the current round, never as parity with the delivered v5.
   - The historic inputs stay missing and are listed by name and known hash in the inventory. Every input of the re-run is a named substitute made now, never a recovered original.
   - The admitted manifest is not edited: its description, written by the request generator, still says the round is not an approved baseline. This decision is the record of that yes.
2. **The two swatches**, generated under this authorization and nothing else.
   - **Route.** One Astra session (`Assets3D Pipeline/mcp/astra_run.py --image-gen`, Codex CLI 0.162.0, provider openai, session `01a1a02d…`). The prompt is the committed `Pipeline/retopology/waist-v5/swatch-prompts.txt`, both blocks unchanged except the save path, with the stored crops `ref-sash-cloth.png` and `ref-hem-tabs.png` attached. No Painter data went to the generator.
   - **Results.** Both are 1,254 px square 8-bit RGB, as the lost originals were. Cloth `ab5168c6…` (median 50/36/29), hem `2d69035f…` (median 76/62/45).
   - **Where they are.** The sandbox refused the save, so each image was copied byte for byte (SHA-256 checked) from the tool's output to the piece's `Textures/Swatches/` as `sash-cloth-BaseColor-v2.png` and `hem-leather-BaseColor-v2.png`. The `v1` names stay the lost originals'; v5.json still pins those.
   - **Provenance.** Each has a private `uzpainter/parity/sash-swatch-provenance/1` record, a generated current-round substitute with its prompt and references by hash. Front W made `T_sash` from them (`c58e8578…`), and an independent repeat is byte-identical.
3. **The base assembly is not needed, and it cannot be regenerated by its pinned route.**
   - **Not needed.** `Assembly/Skeletal-Mage-TunicCoverage-v1.blend` is used by `drive.py` (geometry generation) in `sashchain.sh` and by `paintrender.py` (the review render after the export) in `round.sh`. The re-run takes its geometry from the preserved piece `.blend` (fronts N and P) and the derived centre. `waist_masks.py sash` reads only the Field and the two swatches.
   - **Not regenerable.** Its committed builder `Assembly/Review/TunicCoverage-v1/Evidence/build_coverage_latest.py` asserts its input `Skeletal-Mage-Assembly-v9-RestoredApprovedTunic.blend` is `896ee8c9…`. The file is now `25842ed8…`, so the script refuses. Its saver's input, `_tmp/astra/tunic-waist-correction-20261008/`, is gone, and the two recorded hashes of the output disagree (`38bed369…` in v5.json, `710f2b40…` in report.json).
   - Because nothing in the golden depends on it, this needs no owner question.
4. **The recording** (Painter lock held, then released). The request was recorded by `record-script` under OCIO on `12.2.0+js-api.1.1.20`, and `envelope check --request` gave `ocio_reference`. It was recorded twice, each time in its own Painter process.
   - In each process, r1, r1-repeat and r2 are byte-identical on all five maps, and the two processes gave the same 15 map blobs.
   - The first run is admitted. The second process's record (`668fbeee…`) is kept in the private store as evidence and is not admitted.
   - Painter's Metallic is byte-identical to the delivered `Metallic_v5` (`537c59bf…`).
5. **EXC-012 version 1** (`parity/exceptions.json`), EXC-010's terms for the Sash.
   - **Tier and domain.** Tier 1, `asset_map` `tier1/pilot/sash-v5-current`, golden `phase2/tier1/sash-v5-current` only. The migration golden and the unrecordable `phase2/tier1/sash-v5` are not covered.
   - **Bound 1.** `max_abs_diff` 0 over `covered_interior`, on every map, the Normal included.
   - **Bound 2.** `max_abs_diff` 0 over `covered`, on every map but the Normal (`excepted_outputs: ["export/25-Faixa-Cintura_Normal"]`, the golden record's output id).
   - So the only covered texels that may differ are the Normal's island-edge texels, and the uncovered (padding) texels of all five maps may differ. A comparison without a coverage mask, with a mask of another size, or naming no output is held to both bounds. No numeric bound is put on the excepted texels (decision 223 §3).
   - **Basis** `owner_scope_split`, `approved_by: owner`, `approved_on: 2026-11-03`.
   - **Defect test.** `tests/parity/test_verdict.py::test_exc012_catches_injected_defects`: EXC-010's seven injected defects on the Sash's golden and outputs.
   - Everything that differs is still published per map and per band (decision 395 §2).
6. **What writes the Sash's maps: the diagnostic measure export**, as for the Wand (decision 395 §4) and the Tunic (decision 398 §4).
   - The Wand's runner rebuilds the Sash with `reopened_compared(..., diagnostic=True)`: two rebuilds through the adapter, and the first one's save/reopen export.
   - Each receipt must be marked `non_conformant` and name only decisions 245's and 259's refusals.
   - **Production is unchanged.** `CheckIslandNormal` and `CheckPadding` still refuse on every asset, and a `non_conformant` export gets no Assets3D manifest (decision 286 §5).
7. **Supersedes in part, for the golden `phase2/tier1/sash-v5-current` only**, exactly what decision 395 §5 superseded for the Wand: decision 259 §2 and §4, decision 245 §1, and decision 286 §2. Decision 255 §3's expected failure no longer applies to the Sash's Tier 1 test. Everywhere else those decisions stand.
8. **Reporting and what stays open.**
   - A Sash pass is reported as "pass under EXC-012, against the current round", never as exact and never as parity with the delivered v5. Its report lists EXC-012 version 1 in `exceptions_relied_on`.
   - EXC-012 is narrowed by new versions as `phase3.1_export-island-padding` makes parts exact, and withdrawn once the Sash passes with no exception.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Restore the round folder.** There is no source to restore it from (decisions 192, 260; the inventory).
- **The migration golden as the basis.** Not taken. It tests the delivered project, not a pipeline round, and its rebuild lacks the lost inputs (decision 300). The owner chose the re-run.
- **Save the generated swatches under the lost `v1` names.** Rejected: the new files would pass as the originals that v5.json pins. The pinned donor reads `v1` paths only inside the private run, through staged, hash-checked copies (front W).
- **Extend EXC-010 or EXC-011 to the Sash.** Rejected: the owner asked for one exception per piece.

## Consequences

- The Sash's Tier 1 test passes only when every covered interior texel is equal on all five maps, every covered texel is equal on the four material maps, and the two rebuilds are byte-identical. It is then a pass under EXC-012.
- `parity/reports/phase2/tier1-sash-v5.json` is written by that run.
- Production export of the Sash stays refused, so it has no conformant UzPainter delivery until `phase3.1_export-island-padding` is exact.
