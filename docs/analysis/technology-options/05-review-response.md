# Response to the Astra Technical Review

Response date: 2026-10-10. Review: `docs/analysis/reviews/technology-options-astra-review.md` (32 findings, 10 missing options, 15 questions). Every finding was checked against primary sources retrieved on 2026-10-10 before any change; one local, read-only check re-read the `.spp` file signatures and sizes. No binaries, encoders, installers or benchmarks were run, so no performance figure was measured.

**Verdict counts:** 27 accepted · 4 partially accepted · 0 rejected · 1 forwarded to architecture (findings 29 and M10 are accepted on the facts; their design decisions are also forwarded).

Verdict meanings: **accepted** = every material claim confirmed by a primary source and applied; **partially accepted** = applied, but one part could not be confirmed (stated in the row) or needs an architecture decision; **forwarded** = a requirement for the architect, not a correction to the technology documents.
