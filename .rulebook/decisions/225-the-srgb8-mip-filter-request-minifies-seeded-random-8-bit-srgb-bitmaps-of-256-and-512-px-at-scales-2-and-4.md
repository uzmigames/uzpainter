# 225. The sRGB8 mip filter request minifies seeded random 8-bit sRGB bitmaps of 256 and 512 px at scales 2 and 4

**Status**: accepted
**Date**: 2026-10-13
**Related Tasks**: phase1.15_compositor-colour-fits

## Context

Decision 219 found that Painter's 8-bit sRGB mip levels are round-to-nearest sRGB8 codes, but three candidate filters (the exact mean of the four decodes, the float32 mean, one hardware bilinear probe at the block centre) build identical chains from `srgb-bitmap-minification`'s ramp. On random content they differ (6 382 of 49 152 level-1 values between the probe and the exact mean). It asked for one owner recording: a random-content 8-bit sRGB bitmap minified by BilinearSharp and BilinearHQ at scale 4 on a square set. The held-out `resample-uv-heldout-*` goldens must stay out of every fit.

## Decision

1. **Request `tier0/semantics/srgb-bitmap-minification-random`**, generated by `recording_requests.srgb_bitmap_minification_random()`. It is `srgb-bitmap-minification`'s layout (the shared `srgb_minification_sequence`, which leaves that admitted golden's manifest unchanged) with new sources:
   - Legacy, the authored 4x2 plane grid at 256 px (square sets), one fill per set with only BaseColor (sRGB8), the bitmap set to sRGB, UV projection with Repeat, rotation 0, offset (0, 0);
   - s0-s3: `srgb8-random-256`; s4-s7: `srgb8-random-512`; per source BilinearHQ at scale 2 and 4, then BilinearSharp at 2 and 4;
   - BaseColor at PNG 16, PNG 8 and OpenEXR 32f, infinite padding, every export step done twice.
2. **The bitmaps** are 8-bit RGB PNGs of seeded pseudo-random codes (`request_fixtures.srgb8_random`: the top byte of a 32-bit LCG, seed 20262190 + size). They are authored here (clean room), and no stem ends in a number from 1001 up (decision 166).
3. **Why two sizes.** The 256 px bitmap at scale 4 repeats the ramp's s1/s3 footprint (4 bitmap texels per set texel, level 1) on random codes, and at scale 2 checks decision 219's lifted level-0 domain on random content. The 512 px bitmap gives 4 texels per set texel at scale 2 and 8 at scale 4, so level 2 is read too (BilinearSharp at LOD 1.5, BilinearHQ at about 1.4), which shows whether level 2 is built from level 1 or from level 0. The cost is the same eight sets as the original golden.
4. **Not recorded in batch H.** At 02:54 a Painter process (started 02:05, project `Zombie_bake`) was running while the Assets3D Painter lock was free. Painter is one shared instance and this was not ours, so the session was not started. The request is ready for the next Painter window (parity/RECORDER.md §11.4).

The model panel was not consulted: decision 219 fixes the content, and the extra size costs nothing.

## Consequences

- Once admitted, this golden separates the sRGB8 mip filter, and the mip-level remainder of decision 219 (BilinearSharp past 2.8 and BilinearHQ past 3 texels per set texel) can be fitted. `srgb-bitmap-minification` s1 and s3 stay the cross-check; the held-out goldens stay unread.
