- **Never exact.** All three pilots pass only under owner-approved exceptions. No pilot is equal
  to Painter on every texel.
- **Not a production delivery.** Every pilot map that G2 compared was written by the diagnostic
  measure export (decision 286), whose receipt says `non_conformant`. UzPainter's production
  export still refuses a UV-islanded texture set: `infinite` padding on islands (decision 259 §4)
  and a virtual Normal on an islanded set (decision 245 §1). The owner of the fix is
  `phase3.1_export-island-padding`. All three pilots are UV-islanded texture sets, so today none
  of them can complete a production export in UzPainter.
- **No render parity.** G2 makes no render claim (gate-g2.md §9). Iray-equivalent rendering is
  `phase3.7_path-tracer` and gate G5.
- **Open required gaps.** The scope manifest lists 265 `release-gap` symbols and 37 of 106
  operation-matrix rows as capability gaps (§2). Decision 019 forbids calling UzPainter a full
  replacement while a required gap is open (OWN-01, OWN-03, PRD §18 item 2).

## 2. The scope (item 2.1)

- **Manifest.** `zig-out/bin/capabilities.json` of the ReleaseFast build of `ba0fa817`, SHA-256
  `9f4d2abc558a43bbdf32bb751b78bb0570e257d4851693ff342cb2b4655a16e5`. This is the manifest G2
  records (gate-g2.md §6), regenerated byte-identical on 2026-11-08.
- **Rule.** A symbol is in scope when its `any` entry is `supported` (Tier 0 evidence,
  `10-python-api.md` §3). `pending-evidence`, `release-gap`, `desktop-only-omitted` and unlisted
  symbols are outside it. The production adapter applies this rule (`adapters/assets3d/scope.py`,
  `MIGRATIONS.md` M13).
- **Listing.** `02-amended-scope.json`, generated by `python -m tools.capabilities.amended_scope`
  from that manifest. It holds the in-scope symbols per module and the counts outside it, the
  operation-matrix rows, and the 23 operation contexts that a supported symbol still refuses
  before any mutation (TIFF, PSD, WebP and J2K export, material layering, arbitrary `.sbsar`,
  unrecorded compositor cases).

| Counted on `any` entries | Symbols |
|---|---|
| `supported` (in scope) | 921 |
| `pending-evidence` | 327 |
| `release-gap` | 265 |
| `desktop-only-omitted` | 15 |

**In scope, by operation family** (the `supported` symbols of `02-amended-scope.json`, by module):
- project create, open, save, `save_as` and mesh reload (`project`: 51 Painter symbols);
- texture sets, stacks, channels and resolutions (`textureset`: 143);
- fill layers, groups, masks, blending, opacity and visibility; fill and Levels effects; UV
  projection (`layerstack`: 153);
- uniform colour and bitmap sources (`source`: 34), colour management (`colormanagement`: 21);
- resource import and listing (`resource`: 34);
- PNG and OpenEXR export (`export`: 11), except UV-islanded sets (see above);
- the matching native `uzpainter` modules (`textureset` 131, `layerstack` 94, `project` 34).

The operation matrix marks 69 of its 106 typed-tool rows `available`. "Available" is not the
same as "in scope". Several available rows rest on `pending-evidence` symbols, and those stay
outside the amended scope:
- the bake tools (`baking.bake_async`);
- the `painter_viewport` capture (`uzpainter.render.capture`);
- the recipe tool (`uzpainter.project.dump_recipe`).

**Outside the scope, so they stay in Painter** (the 37 gap rows, owners named in
`02-amended-scope.json`):
- smart materials and smart masks;
- resource and asset search;
- procedurals, generators and filters;
- anchors, instances and references;
- compare-mask and colour-selection effects;
- material sources, vector and font sources;
- 3D projections (triplanar, planar, spherical, cylindrical, warp, UV-set to UV-set);
- UV-tile workflows;
- the TGA, BMP, JPEG and DDS writers, and TIFF, PSD, WebP and J2K;
- Iray captures (`painter_iray`).

Every `pending-evidence` capability is outside the scope too (327 symbols).

**What the adapter lets through today**, measured on the pipeline's own code against this
manifest (a static reading, `MIGRATIONS.md` M13):

## 4. What the owner must do

1. **Read §1 and §2 and decide.** Is the amendment worth signing now? Today no pilot can complete
   a production round in UzPainter: islanded export is refused, and HEAD's `sync` is out of
   scope. Signing now authorizes only the proven scope, and practical use waits on
   `phase3.1_export-island-padding` and on `Resource.retrieve`.
2. **Approve or change the text of §3**, then sign it in your own words. A model panel, an agent
   message or silence does not count (decision 259 §6).
3. **Choose the scope manifest.** Either sign §3 as written, pinned to `9f4d2abc…` (the G2
   manifest), or ask for a later manifest; then §2 and `02-amended-scope.json` are regenerated
   from it before signing.
4. **Decide the route-token gap** (decision 144, `MIGRATIONS.md` M8). The UzPainter client
   ignores `UZ_ROUTE_TOKEN`, so a new-layout character (`Creatures/<Family>/<Name>/Project/`,
   such as ChibiKnight) is not guarded by the client the way Assets3D's MCP client guards it.
   Either keep UzPainter production rounds to legacy-layout pieces until the adapter checks the
   token, or authorize closing M8 first.
5. **Then the agents do the rest; nothing is done before your signature:**
   - record the signed text, the manifest SHA-256 and your approval as a rulebook decision
     (follow-up of decision 026; item 2.3);
   - apply §3 in Assets3D under its collaboration protocol (item 2.4, §5);
   - cite the decision in the G3 gate record.

  fronts have uncommitted files in that checkout. Check the commit with `git show HEAD` and the
  tree count. Delete nothing.
