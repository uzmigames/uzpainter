Under an injected `VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST`, the pending readbacks fail with `device_removed {backend: vulkan, reason: VK_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST (-4)}` and the readback holds no staging buffer. Its memory stays with the abandoned device until the process ends (decision 310 §6), as every object of a failed Vulkan device does. Behind a stalled queue a readback times out and keeps its staging buffer while the GPU may still write it; the drain frees that buffer once the copy has completed. These are the outcomes of the DX12 `readback-drain` and `readback-failure` cases.
