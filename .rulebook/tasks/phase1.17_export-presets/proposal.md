# Proposal: phase1.17_export-presets

## Why
The pipeline exports through named presets (`defaultExportPreset`) and through its inline five-map configuration; UzPainter ships its own hand-written presets in the documented export-configuration form, defined from Painter's documented export behaviour and confirmed against private observed outputs, never decoded from Adobe preset data (EXP-01, EXP-08 subset, CLR-01, CLR-06, LIC-03). Implements D7 (decision 009: Painter-compatible JSON compiled into a validated plan) with owner answer Q10 (decision 023) in `docs/architecture/08-export.md` §1–§6; Phase 1 covers the plan compiler, padding/resize/dither, PNG8/16, EXR, the PBR, Blender and inline presets, generation-directory publication and receipts, while the Unreal, glTF and UzEngine presets and the TGA/BMP/JPEG/DDS writers follow in P3-UNREAL and P3-UZE. Child of `phase1.18_export-engine` (P1-EXPORT), covering `docs/architecture/08-export.md` §4 for the presets named in the P1-EXPORT Outputs (PBR, Blender, inline) and `docs/licensing-and-clean-room.md` §1 item 7.

## What Changes
- `assets/export-presets/pbr-metallic-roughness.json` (answers to `PBR Metallic Roughness`), `assets/export-presets/blender-principled-bsdf.json` (answers to `Blender (Principled BSDF)`, the pipeline's preset), `assets/export-presets/assets3d-inline-five-map.json` (the Assets3D inline five-map preset whose provenance is the pipeline's `painter_export.py`).
- `assets/export-presets/<preset>.provenance.json` per preset: `{preset, version, author, date, public sources (page and section), observed-output fixtures (SHA-256 of the private records), packing decisions with their source, parity status}`.
- `src/export/preset_aliases.{h,cpp}`: compatibility alias table so Painter preset names keep working in `defaultExportPreset`; aliases are internal only (CLR-06).
- No `.spexp` file is parsed or shipped; no preset is generated by serialising a readback. `Unreal Engine (Packed)`, `glTF PBR Metal Roughness`, `Mesh Maps`, the hat v8 SpecularWeight variant and the UzEngine preset are delivered by later packages.

## Dependencies
- DAG input of the parent: `phase1.14_compositor-core`.
- Sibling prerequisites: `phase1.15_export-plan-compiler`, `phase1.16_export-processing-encoders`.
- Parent: `phase1.18_export-engine`. Consumer: `phase1.25_pilot-wand` (export through the pipeline preset).

## Acceptance
Each shipped preset passes its output-parity test against the private oracle (files Painter writes for that preset on a UzmiGames fixture project) before it ships, and its provenance record is complete.

## Impact
- Affected specs: `export`
- Affected code: `assets/export-presets/`, `src/export/preset_aliases.*`, `tests/export/`
- Breaking change: NO
- User benefit: The pipeline's preset names and inline configuration produce Painter-equal files from clean-room presets.
