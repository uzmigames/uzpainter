  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 327): `src/procedural/snapshot_backend.{h,cpp}`. `Load` reads a project's entries through their plane bindings and refuses a malformed, foreign or mis-keyed entry and a plane that is not the signature's output. `CanEvaluate` is the exact signature hash only (and a tile inside the output); `Refusal` names the first differing field; `Stored` returns the recorded bytes, `Evaluate` the same texels as float32 (codes / 255 or 65535, halves widened exactly), a tile as a copy. `Status` is `unavailable` for every asset: snapshots certify nothing (decision 222).
