# 100. The Phase 1 recording requests are merged into one specification per recording and prepared for two sessions

**Status**: accepted
**Date**: 2026-10-12
**Related Tasks**: phase1.16_export-processing-encoders, phase1.17_export-presets, phase1.18_export-engine, phase1.20_pyapi-facade, phase1.21_pyapi-dispatcher-lifecycle, phase1.22_pyapi-cli-capabilities, phase1.25_pilot-wand

## Context

Two requests ask for the same Painter observations:

- the recording plan of 2026-10-12 (R1-R10, export processing, presets, OCIO encoding, the Wand v1 Tier 1 golden and extra API probes);
- the phase1.16 request R-090 (`parity/reports/phase1/export-processing-encoders.md` §5, probes P1-P10).

R1-R4 overlap P1-P10. The next Painter session must record everything in one pass, so every recording needs one authored manifest now, with its fixtures and recorder support (decision 099 gives the form).

## Decision

One golden per recording; R-090's probes are folded into the plan's recordings. Every Tier 0 golden is a requested golden under `parity/envelopes/painter-12.1.5_js-api.1.1.20/requests/tier0/semantics/`, generated by `tools/parity/tier0_semantics/recording_requests.py` (`python -m tools.parity.tier0_semantics write`). Unless stated, export steps run twice in a row in the same session (R-090: run-to-run noise; the recorder keys such maps by step).

Deviations from the requests, stated:

- R1 uses four islands, not three. The fourth is turned 30 degrees so that its borders cross texels diagonally, and the 1-, 3- and 20-texel gaps all exist.
- R4 runs its float probes through proven routes: two LinearDodge copies (up to 2) and SignedAddition onto an empty stack (down to -0.5). Uniform colours outside [0, 1] are not used, because a raising state step would refuse the whole golden.
- R6 fills only the channels the Alpha-blend template is certain to have. Emissive is left out, and its export, if the template has the channel, shows the empty constant.
- R-090 names predefined PBR Metallic Roughness. On disk it is a starter resource preset (`resource://starter_assets/PBR Metallic Roughness`). The predefined presets are listed and their output maps captured, so they are recorded without knowing their names in advance.
- The hat v8 variant of R8 is not prepared: no hat v8 export script exists in Assets3D.
- Golden ids use the existing families (decision 099), not the plan's `tier0/export/*`.

## Alternatives Considered

- Separate goldens per R-090 probe: rejected. That gives more sessions' worth of project setup for the same observations.
- Recording R-090 on its own `grid2x2` project: its s0-s3 content is folded into R4's eight sets.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Session L records 13 Tier 0 export goldens and the 8 R10 probes (phase1-delete-node, -resource-update, -save-modes, -create-settings, -reload-mesh, -reload-resources, -execute-when-not-busy, -engine-computations, the last one last: its export with engine computations disabled may not return). Session O records 2 Tier 0 goldens and R9. The order and commands are in `parity/RECORDER.md` §11.
- phase1.16 items 2.2-2.6, phase1.17 items 2.1-3.1, phase1.20's OCIO items and phase1.25 each get the recordings they wait on.

### Negative
- `export-bit-depth` is large (378 steps). A state step Painter refuses refuses the whole golden; the runbook says what to do then.
