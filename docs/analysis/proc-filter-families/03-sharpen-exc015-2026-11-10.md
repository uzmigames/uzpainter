# Sharpen under EXC-015, 2026-11-10 (decision 430)

`filter/sharpen@2` was judged under EXC-015 by the verdict on its native CPU lane, over every compared texel (decision 423 §1) of the batch `parity/manifests/phase3/proc-cert/filter__sharpen@2/`. The test is `tests/procedural/test_proc_filter_families.py::test_sharpen_v2_passes_under_exc015_on_its_batch`, and it runs against the private store. Only counts appear below.

## 1. Verdict per case

| Set | Cases | Outcome |

The native lane equals the certified candidate (`tools/parity/proc_filters/sharpen_candidate.py`) on every served case. The `sharpen2` native case checks it bit for bit on synthetic codes at the six served intensities, and the native outputs reproduce the harness's exact and residual split.

## 2. The interior residual is not a midpoint residual

The 0.63 and 2.71 cases were diagnosed on counts only (`k + intensity x L` taken exactly from the decimal intensity, L the wrapped four-neighbour Laplacian of the content codes):

| Case | Residual texels | On exact midpoints | Off them: k + intensity x L, distance from a half | Painter's direction off them |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.63, Tunic v10 1024 | 1 019 | 226 | 0.01 to 0.05 code above | down (793 of 793) |
| 0.63, knob cube 2048 | 11 368 | 2 877 | 0.01 to 0.05 code above | down (8 491 of 8 491) |
| 2.71, Tunic v10 1024 | 127 | 30 | 0.01 below to 0.07 above | down 93, up 4 |
| 2.71, knob cube 2048 | 714 | 189 | 0.01 below to 0.07 above | down 504, up 21 |

- |L| on the off-midpoint residual texels ranges from 169 to 56 323.
- A quantised intensity does not explain them. Float16, 1/256, 1/255, 1/1024 and 1/4096 intensities differ on more texels than the decimal intensity does: 13 082, 16 112, 30 748, 13 082 and 13 082 on the Tunic 0.63 case, against 1 019; 120 519, 150 381, 380 254, 120 519 and 120 519 on the knob cube 0.63 case, against 11 368.

Decision 426 §2's "every residual texel is ... at an exact midpoint" therefore holds at 0.25 only. EXC-015's predicate does not cover the interior residual, and widening it to a near-midpoint band would be a new exception for the owner.

## 3. Consequence

- EXC-015 version 2 keeps version 1's approved domain and bounds and corrects the evidence.
- The record serves intensity 0, 0.2, 0.25, 0.4, 1 and 4 only, and refuses 0.63 and 2.71 before any work.
- Item 2.10 of `phase3.3_procedural-families` keeps the interior rounding open, with the float-to-UNORM16 write measurement and a new held-out set.
