## Decision

1. `resample-uv-minify` and `resample-uv-impulse` follow the `resample-uv` structure: grid2x2, User0-7 RGB16, one fill per channel stacked upwards, and `Factors` projection. Per set: s0 BilinearHQ with Repeat, s1 BilinearSharp with Repeat, s2 BilinearHQ with RepeatNone, s3 BilinearSharp with RepeatNone. Scales and offsets are as requested. Rotation is 0 everywhere except minify User7 (30). The impulse User7 has no rotation.
2. `impulse64.png` is generated by `tools/parity/tier0_semantics/fixtures.py`, with (x, y) as PNG column and row 0 as the first row. A test checks every texel.
3. `group-coverage-order` is built as B1. On the L channels (User1 L32F, User5 L8), grey-a takes the place of field-a and grey-b the place of field-b: each grey fixture equals the red ramp of its field. The RGB channels use the fields as requested. Uniform 0 is set in the Working space.
4. All three are manifests of the current envelope only (`tier0/semantics/`), recorded twice (same session, after save and reopen) and admitted to the store. Fitting stays with phase1.9 and phase1.10; their task files are not edited here.

## Alternatives Considered

- Feed the L channels field-a and field-b literally (rejected by the panel majority: Painter's RGB-to-L conversion would be a second unknown in a diagnostic of rounding order).
- Rotate the impulse User7 like the minify User7 (rejected: the request lists only offsets for it).

## Consequences

Phase1.9 can fit the minification kernels from `resample-uv-minify` and `resample-uv-impulse`, holding out `resample-uv` User6. Phase1.10 has its diagnostic golden. Its L8 and L32F channels observe the coverage tie on grey inputs, not on an RGB-to-L conversion.
