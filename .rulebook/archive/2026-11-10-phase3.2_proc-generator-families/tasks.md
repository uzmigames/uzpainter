- [x] 2.2 Implement the Curvature, Position, World Space Normals and Ambient Occlusion generators reading mesh maps by usage
  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 329): `src/procedural/generators.{h,cpp}`: four `IHlslFamily` families registered from their library records (`RegisterGeneratorFamilies`), each one kernel pass shaping one mesh-map signal (signed curvature, position axis, signed world-normal axis, occlusion 1 - ao) with Peachey's boxstep and smoothstep, plus a CPU reference from the primitive library. - [x] 2.3 Implement Dirt and Metal Edge Wear from curvature/AO edge and cavity detection plus the shared noise and morphology primitives
  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 329): Dirt (cavity clamp(1 - 2c) and occlusion, after Miller 1994, Hsu and Wong 1995) and Metal Edge Wear (convex edges less occluded wear, after Paquette, Poulin and Drettakis 2001): three passes each, the base mask, a disk dilation (`prim::Dilate` / HLSL `Morph`) and an fBm Perlin 2002 breakup over the position map seeded by the request, then a boxstep. Evidence: `tests/procedural/test_proc_generator_families.py` `test_dirt_and_edge_wear_follow_their_formulas` (13 checks, CPU: formulas at the defaults and an edit, seed dependence, monotone spread, edge detection equal to the Curvature generator, tiles) and the DX12 case; log `tmp/scratch/proc-gen/e-pytest-generators.log`.
