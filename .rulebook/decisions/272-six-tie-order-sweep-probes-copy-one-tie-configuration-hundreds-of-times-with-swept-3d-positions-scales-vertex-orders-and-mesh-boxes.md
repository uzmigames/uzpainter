# 272. Six tie-order sweep probes copy one tie configuration hundreds of times with swept 3D positions, scales, vertex orders and mesh boxes

**Status**: accepted
**Date**: 2026-10-19
**Related Tasks**: phase1.25_pilot-wand, phase1.18_export-engine, phase1.16_export-processing-encoders

## Context

On 2026-10-18 the owner ruled that the Wand must match Painter everywhere, padding included. Exact `infinite` padding on UV islands (item 2.7 of `phase1.25_pilot-wand`) is the critical path.

**What is known.** Every ring value is an FMA lerp chain over quantised 1/d shares, bit-exact given the order of the taps (decisions 267, 268 and 270). The one unknown left is the **tie order**: which of two exactly equal shares leads the chain.

**What has been ruled out** (decisions 267, 270 and 271):
- every reconstruction of the per-texel position arithmetic tried so far: barycentric forms, interpolation orders, mixed tap and ring recipes, plane equations, derivative extrapolation, reduced-precision storage, packed keys and fixed neighbour orders;
- the best rule (decision 267's own-triangle comparator) is exact on tie, r1 and r2, but gets 1,150 of 1,661 on the atlas and 1,014 on the moved atlas.

**What the data say about the order:**
- it depends only on 3D rounding. It is identical at 512, 1024 and 2048 px, and the moved atlas (same normalised values, other rounding) changes 368 of 1,661;
- on the one-texel row it switches at the float32 binade x′ = 0.25;
- seam own-diagonal ties break both ways even on exact r2 geometry;
- the V-gap position choice of decision 270 also looks like a tie-break.

**Why new probes.** The atlas has one copy of each configuration, and its 3D layout is isometric to its UV layout. So position, binade and box effects are mixed together, and the world binades sit on the normalised binades (the box is exactly [−256, 256]). What is missing is the same configuration under controlled 3D changes.

## Decision

### 1. Many copies of one configuration, with only the 3D placement varied

**The meshes.** Each mesh is one texture set, `uzp_tie_sweep`, holding hundreds of copies of a few UV configurations (*units*). They are generated by `request_fixtures.strip_copies`, `seam_copies` and `tie_sweep`.
- **Copies.** Every copy is its own 3D piece and its own UV island. Its sub-texel UV phase is the same in every copy; only its whole-texel slot changes.
- **Spacing.** Copies are packed in shelves, at least 7 texels apart in UV, and their 3D boxes are disjoint.
- **No texel centre lies on any triangle edge,** split diagonals included. A test checks this, and also the covered-texel count of each copy.
- **3D placement.** A unit's local 3D is isometric, (column, 0, row) cm. It is then scaled per axis, turned about +y by 0, 90, 180 or 270 degrees, and moved so that its centre lands on a target.
- **Stacking in y.** Copies whose 3D boxes would meet, such as the k-ulp translations of one base, are stacked in y. The copies are flat, so y rounding is far below the squared-distance comparison's last bit under every tested arithmetic. The y-control copies (F3) test that.
- **Positions** are written as exact decimals of their float32 values. **UVs** are dyadic.

**The units:**

| Unit | UV configuration | Ties it carries |
|---|---|---|
| `row-8`, `row-16` | one quad, 8 or 16 texels × 1, corners 1/4 texel in (as the atlas rows) | straight-edge ties between the ±u diagonals, 6 or 14 per long side |
| `column-8`, `column-16` | the same, transposed | ±v ties |
| `seam` | A 8 × 8 texels and B 4 × 4 at ratio 2, turned 90 degrees, as P-ramp r2. Both seam edges are on texel boundaries as at r2-p0, and the other edges are 1/8 texel off | seam own-diagonal ties on ring A (6 per copy) and far/far ties on ring B |
| `vgap` | two triangles of pole-4's fan at half-size 4, sharing the spoke apex–rim in 3D and only the base vertex in UV | the V-gap position choice of decision 270 |

### 2. The sweeps

**Strips family** (304 copies, 5,824 straight-edge ties):

| Group | What varies | Copies |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | 16-texel strips straddling 17 boundaries, along x and along z, rows and columns, on two lines each. The boundaries are x′ = 0, ±1/16, ±1/8, ±1/4, ±1/2, ±3/4 of the centred box (fl(p/h) = 0.25, 0.5 and 1 in the off-centre box) and x′ = ±1/8, ±1/4, ±1/2 of box320 | 136 |
| F2 | k-ulp translations, k = 0..15 across and 1..15 along, of `row-8` at bases A (100, 52), B (48, 100) and C (−150, −44) cm, and of `column-8` at A. Each base keeps its unit inside one float32 binade in all three boxes | 124 |
| F3 | the y control: base A unshifted at y = ±40, ±44 and ±48 cm, in distant UV slots | 6 |
| F4 | uniform scales 0.5 to 3 (1.7 non-dyadic), and per-axis scales of x or z by 0.75, 1.5, 1.7 and 2 | 15 |
| F5 | every diagonal × vertex rotation × face order: 11 rows at A and 12 columns at B | 23 |

**Seams family** (148 copies):

| Group | What varies | Copies |
|---|---|---|

| G2 | seams folded 90 degrees and atan(4/3) degrees down, two turns at two positions | 8 |

### 3. Three mesh boxes per family

Each family is written three times. The UVs and faces are identical; only the box-related positions change.

| Box | Positions | What it isolates |
|---|---|---|
| `centred` | Two anchor triangles fix the box at exactly [−256, 256] cm in x and z and [−64, 64] in y, so p′ = p/256 is exact | The per-texel arithmetic on exact vertices |
| `offcentre` | Every vertex moved by (256, 0, 256) cm, rounded to float32. The box is [0, 512] | The same normalised values reached through fl(p/h) ∈ [0, 2] and fl(c/h) = 1. It separates decision 261's two-step normalisation from (p − c)/h and from world-space arithmetic. Small ulp steps collapse at 356 cm, which is documented, not avoided |
| `box320` | Only the anchors move, to ±320 | The normalised binades (x′ = 0.25 at 80 cm) no longer sit on world binades (64 cm), so F1's two boundary sets tell world-space from normalised-space rounding |

### 4. The requests

**Six requests** are generated by `recording_requests.padding_tie_sweep_probes()`: `export-padding-tie-sweep-{strips,seams}-{centred,offcentre,box320}`.
- They use decision 266's `kernel_probe` sequence at 512 px, with the existing 512 px kernel bitmaps: the one-hot slots read each tap's weight directly, so the leading diagonal is read with no model.
- The void rules are decision 266's.
- **No new bitmaps.** There are six new meshes under `parity/fixtures/tier0-semantics/meshes/tie-sweep-*.obj`, 74 to 109 KB each.
- **Not requested:**
  - resolutions other than 512, because the order does not depend on them (decision 267);
  - a UV-shuffled twin, because F3 varies the UV slot at fixed 3D;
  - the pyramid V-gap, whose open item is the tap points, not the order (decision 270, 4(b)).

**The model panel was not consulted.** The coordinator fixed the goal, the budget and the sweep dimensions, and the choices left (bases, boundaries, counts) are parameters, not forks.

**None is void:**
- every template capture shows User0 to User7 as RGB32F;
- every r1 `passthrough` equals its source bitmaps on every non-zero texel;
- the covered texels (`infinite` equal to the sources on all 24 channels) are exactly the designed 3,520 (strips) and 8,528 (seams).

**Stability.**
- Every `infinite` export is byte-identical across both steps of r1 and of r2, and between r1 and r2.
- Both r1 `passthrough` steps are identical.
- As on every earlier kernel probe, r2's `passthrough` equals its `infinite`.
- So, like every earlier P-ramp, P-tie and kernel probe, each counts as unstable only on its `passthrough` steps after reopen. The stability report, regenerated without fits, now covers 240 goldens: 137 stable, 103 unstable.

### 6. First read (strips only, one-hot slots of r1 `infinite`)

This is a check that the probes carry the signal. It is not a fit. The script stays in private scratch, because it reads oracle values.

- **Every tie is readable.** All 5,824 straight-edge ties per box have unequal diagonal weights, so none is a tie in the output.
- **Leads toward +u or +v.** 3,310 in the centred box, 3,424 in offcentre and 3,037 in box320.
- **Orders changed against centred.** offcentre changes 204 and box320 changes 2,125. A box change that leaves the vertices alone moves far more orders than one that keeps the normalised values.
- **The y control holds.** Under all three boxes, the six F3 copies give exactly the orders of their base, so the y stacking and the UV slot are neutral there.
- **The ulp sweeps carry the signal.** At base A, a one-ulp move across x changes the row strip's 12 orders, with no two neighbouring k equal. Moves along z change only a few texels.
