## 7. Native Normal calculation and encoding (2026-10-20)
- Item 2.2: the native Embree baker shares its primary hit with AO, interpolates the high mesh's authored normal, and applies the existing inverse MikkTSpace frame. PerVertex is the default; PerFragment is explicit. OpenGL uses the Phase 0 prototype's float32 quantization order; DirectX complements the encoded green code, including 32768 to 32767. Negative Z is preserved. Non-hit texels remain zero with separate class evidence; singular/non-finite frames fail the job without publishing a result.
- `LowParts` computes MikkTSpace on complete objects before selecting a material and retains tangents per triangle corner. Caller rounding/FTZ/DAZ state cannot change generated tangents and is restored. Native complete-object inputs generate missing tangents before the job. Normal-only requests ignore AO settings, cast no secondary rays and leave AO maps empty. Combined requests retain the AO maps. AO-only canonical bytes keep `UZPBAKE3`; Normal uses `UZPBAKE4` with usage/mode/format metadata.
- Explicit native boundary: SubSampling 1, DilationWidth 0, identity transforms on imported low objects. Other values are refused before starting a job. Item 2.1 remains open until the outstanding domains are established; no Python/MCP baking defaults, metadata or capabilities are promoted. Both native usages remain `pending-evidence` with individual owner tasks.

## 11. Native Tier 2b comparison and fits (2026-11-08)
- Tooling: `uzp-bake-feasibility-tests bake-files` (`tests/bake/bake_feasibility_test.cpp`) bakes one texture set from mesh files as `PrepareBakes` does (the low through `ImportMeshFile`, the highs through the bake mesh reader, the request from `BakingParameters` after the golden's `--set` steps); `--dump-low` writes the low parts with their MikkTSpace corner tangents and `--box-size-metric` names a hypothesis for relative distances. `tools/bake/normal_tier2b.py` replays each `tier2b/bakes/normal-*` golden's sequence through it and compares hit/coverage first, then every 16-bit code, r1 and r2 (counts only). A golden with Painter's relative distances is baked under both box-size metrics and stands only if they agree. `tools/bake/normal_fit.py` recomputes the hemisphere and sphere goldens under named candidates. Tests: `tests/bake/test_normal_tier2b.py`.
- Implemented natively (the core every usage shares): (1) texel samples follow decision 408's rasterisation (float32 device coordinates, 1/256-texel vertices, the top-left rule, the snapped texel-space triangle's weights); (2) `SmoothNormals` ("Average normals", Painter's default on) is read: the distance-based primary ray runs along the interpolated averaged normal (per part, the normalised sum of the distinct normals at each vertex position); `keys_not_read` is now `SkewFaceBehavior` only. Docs: `06-baking.md` section 3.2; this task's spec.
- Results (r1 = r2 in every golden; hit/coverage equal on all seven):
  - `normal-plane`: exact (65536 hit texels).
  - `normal-bevelled-cube`: exact (108192 hit texels). Without (2) Painter hits 2136 more texels near the cube's hard edges (native centre misses); with it, hits and codes are equal on every texel. (1) changes nothing here.
  - `normal-hemisphere-per-vertex`: 971 of 150140 hit texels differ (598 by one code, 373 by more); before (1), 36034 differed. `per-fragment`: 1005 differ. Every one-code texel lies within 0.0066 code of a rounding boundary (float32-level arithmetic): float64 frame arithmetic leaves 490 (per-vertex) and 508 (per-fragment), FMA-ordered float32 variants do not improve. The large differences are texels whose primary ray meets a high facet edge exactly (centres on the low's mirror planes): Painter picks the other facet, and neither low-triangle tie rules, face, radial or averaged ray directions nor float32 ray construction reproduce its choice.
  - `normal-sphere` (low as high, Painter's relative distances): 74415 of 241782 hit texels differ by one code, the sign of the residue around zero (Painter R = 32767 on 22092 texels, G on 69214). The native map depends on the box-size metric (Embree's hit barycentrics); Painter's does not.
