## 2. Implementation
- [x] 2.1 Define the snapshot signature with every field of `05-procedural-content.md` §4 and its canonical hash
  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 327): `src/procedural/seam.{h,cpp}` `SignatureJson`: asset id, version, effective parameters, preset, seed, output {id, resolution, format, colour space, channels}, image inputs by hash with their specs, mesh class and UV tiles, and the project settings that affect evaluation (`EvaluationSettings`, admission.h). `Signature` is its canonical JSON (MANIFEST.md §2), `SignatureHash` the SHA-256 of that text; numbers compare by value, non-finite numbers are tagged objects, the tile is excluded. `ProceduralRequest` and `UseContext` gain `outputId` and `settings`. Evidence: `uzp-procedural-tests snapshot` through `tests/procedural/test_proc_snapshot_backend.py::test_snapshot_backend` (54 checks, CPU).
- [x] 2.2 Implement `SnapshotBackend.status/canEvaluate/evaluate`: exact signature match only, else `unavailable`; no resampling or extrapolation; a snapshot is migration data only: it never counts as calibration or held-out evidence and never widens an asset's certified editable domain (decision 222)
  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 327): `src/procedural/snapshot_backend.{h,cpp}`. `Load` reads a project's entries through their plane bindings and refuses a malformed, foreign or mis-keyed entry and a plane that is not the signature's output. `CanEvaluate` is the exact signature hash only (and a tile inside the output); `Refusal` names the first differing field; `Stored` returns the recorded bytes, `Evaluate` the same texels as float32 (codes / 255 or 65535, halves widened exactly), a tile as a copy. `Status` is `unavailable` for every asset: snapshots certify nothing (decision 222). A parameter edit of a snapshot-served source is refused before the mutation commits. Evidence: `uzp-procedural-tests snapshot` through `tests/procedural/test_proc_snapshot_backend.py::test_snapshot_backend` (54 checks, CPU).
- [x] 2.3 Store snapshot rasters as `.uzp` raster planes with `plane_id` and their signatures in the manifest; include them in save/reopen, `inspect` and `export-bundle`
  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 327): `src/store/snapshots.{h,cpp}`: the manifest's `procedural_snapshots` entries {signature, project_uuid, plane, plane_versions}, keyed by signature hash; the planes are project-level `.uzp` raster planes (no set, stack, channel or tile) read by every plane rule (`ManifestPaintPlanes`), so save/reopen, revert, compaction, integrity and the bundle carry them. `ProjectStore::RecordSnapshot` stages one; `inspect` lists them. Evidence: `uzp-procedural-tests snapshot` through `tests/procedural/test_proc_snapshot_backend.py::test_snapshot_backend` (54 checks, CPU): both snapshots bit-exact after save/reopen and after `export-bundle` (PNG for u16, EXR for half) and the bundle import; regressions `tests/store/test_project_store.py -k planes_` and `tests/store/test_store_inspect.py` (inspect, verify, bundle, census).
- [x] 2.4 Refuse copying a snapshot into another project and keep snapshots out of every shipped asset folder
  - Note (2026-10-20, decision 327): The commit refuses an entry recorded for another project or stored under another key (`CheckSnapshots`), as does the bundle import; `CheckIntegrity` reports it (`snapshot`) and `SnapshotBackend::Load` refuses it; a copied `ProceduralRef` carries no snapshot. `test_no_snapshot_in_shipped_asset_folders` fails on any `.uzp`, bundle or `procedural_snapshots` entry in the asset folders build.zig installs. Evidence: `uzp-procedural-tests snapshot` through `tests/procedural/test_proc_snapshot_backend.py::test_snapshot_backend` (54 checks, CPU).
