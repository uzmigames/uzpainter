# Local image-to-3D on the RTX 4090: feasible at 1024^3, parity with Meshy 7.1 unproven, fusion not fixed
**Source**: manual
**Date**: 2026-10-13
**Tags**: analysis:tripo-meshy-local-generation, image-to-3d, assets3d, gpu, meshy, tripo
Analysis 2026-10-13 (docs/analysis/tripo-meshy-local-generation/). Tripo and Meshy do not disclose their current architectures. Tripo's open papers (TripoSG: 1.5-4B rectified-flow DiT over an SDF VAE, CLIP+DINOv2 conditioning; TripoSF: sparse voxels up to 1024^3) and the public literature (TRELLIS.2, Hunyuan3D 2.5 LATTICE 10B, Direct3D-S2, Sparc3D) share one recipe: 3D VAE latent, flow transformer, isosurface extraction, simplification. Meshy 7.1 Ultra 4K = 4096^3 grid, up to 80M raw triangles, then simplified; the pipeline receives 0.16-1.8M. MIT-licensed open models that fit one 4090: TRELLIS.2-4B (512-1536^3, stated >=24 GB, Linux only), Pixal3D (TRELLIS.2 backbone, the only open multi-view, --low_vram 1024), Direct3D-S2 (1024^3 ~24 GB, geometry only). Hunyuan3D 2.1 runs on Windows but its licence excludes the EU, UK and South Korea, and the owner had excluded Hunyuan. On this machine only ~20 GB VRAM is free (the 4090 drives one display and desktop apps); Ubuntu-24.04 is WSL1 (no CUDA), and Docker has the nvidia runtime -> use one CUDA container per model. Local generation does NOT fix the main pipeline defect: parts that touch fuse into one watertight isosurface. Parity with Meshy needs the phase-2 bake-off against the stored Meshy highs.
